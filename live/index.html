Durak

The Russian card game with transfers. 36 cards, trumps from the bottom of the deck, and the last player holding cards loses. Click a card to play it. The buttons under the table take the cards or end your turn.

Play a friend online

A defender who hasn't beaten anything yet can lay a card of the same rank and pass the whole attack on. That works only while nothing on the table is beaten, the other player has enough cards to answer, and there are fewer than six cards down.

The online table has no server. One player hosts and shares a six-letter code, and the two browsers talk directly over WebRTC. The host runs every move through the same rules function and sends the whole state to both players.
