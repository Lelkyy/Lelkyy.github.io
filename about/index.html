About

I'm a CSE-AI student at UC San Diego. I like building things that have to be right about the real world: a public record of drone strikes that reads the news every morning, the payload radio for a CanSat that flew, an antenna that is slowly becoming a radar, and an extension that grades every chess move as it's played.

Most of it is Python, with C++ where speed matters and JavaScript when it has to run in a browser. I've written two research papers, one on neural scaling laws and one on how globular clusters evaporate.

Away from the keyboard I play chess and classical guitar and do target shooting.
