Drone Strike Map

Every reported drone and missile strike in the Russia-Ukraine war, day by day, with the outlet behind each figure.

The problem

Strike reports are spread across dozens of outlets, in several languages, with different counting rules. A week later most are hard to find, and aggregators drop the sourcing.

What I built

Scrapers for 51 sources in 3 languages run every 30 minutes, with rule-based and LLM extraction. Each claim is stored in SQLite by day, and a Leaflet map steps through the days.

Claims are never merged. When two outlets give two figures for the same strike, both stay, each with its source.

What went wrong

The two sides do not publish the same kind of number. Ukraine reports launches, while Moscow reports interceptions, often for a whole region and several nights at once. Where a Russian figure can't be split into single nights it is shown as a range, because the sources don't support anything more precise.

Feeds stop answering without saying so, and the record can look complete while one source adds nothing. A feed that fails to answer is now marked silent. Nothing already stored changes, and that feed simply stops adding to the record until it comes back.

An empty map reads as peace. Small, local events arrive late or not at all, so blank space means nothing was read there, not that nothing happened. A day without a published tally is the same trap. The page now says both, and the record never fills a gap with an estimate.

Result

Live at dronestrikemap.com with 2,000+ monthly users, and a public REST API of 13 endpoints, served from a Linux server behind nginx with TLS and rate limiting.
