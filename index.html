Drone Strike Map

Open the live map ↗

The Drone Strike Map records every reported drone and missile strike in the Russia-Ukraine war, with the outlet behind each figure.

Scrapers read 51 sources in three languages every 30 minutes, using rule-based and LLM extraction. The data is served through a public REST API with 13 endpoints, from a Linux server behind nginx. More than 2,000 people use the map each month.

Research

Independent research written during my A Levels. Both papers can be read in full on this site, and the code for each is public.

EPQ · A* · April 2025

Investigating Neural Scaling Laws in a Multilayer Perceptron

I wrote a neural network library from scratch in NumPy and trained it in many configurations to see how model size, learning rate and training time affect performance. The best model reached 98.51% accuracy on MNIST. The library is published on PyPI as elkwork, and the paper runs to 57 pages.

Read the paper

Physics · June 2025

How Does the Primordial Binary Fraction Affect the Survival Time of an Open Cluster?

Using a 2D N-body simulation, I compared clusters with 0%, 8%, 16% and 24% of their stars in primordial binaries. Clusters with more binaries kept more of their mass and survived longer, consistent with binaries acting as an internal energy source. The work was written up as a paper and a poster.
