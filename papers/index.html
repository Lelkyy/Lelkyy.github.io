Investigating Neural Scaling Laws in a Multilayer Perceptron

April 2025 · 57 pages

How do model size, learning rate and training epochs change what a multilayer perceptron can do? I built one from scratch in NumPy and measured each configuration on MNIST.

Bigger models scored better, with diminishing returns. More epochs made large models overfit. The best learning rate depended on model size.

How Does the Primordial Binary Fraction Affect the Survival Time of an Open Cluster in the Galactic Disk?

June 2025 · 5 pages and a poster

A 2D N-body simulation ran clusters from identical starts with 0%, 8%, 16% and 24% of stars in primordial binaries, ten seeds each.

More binaries meant more mass kept and slower core collapse. The effect grew steadily with the binary fraction.
